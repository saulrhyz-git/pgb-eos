Business Unit Integrator User Guide
Primary Group of Builders · Version 1.0

1. Introduction

The EOS Executive Dashboard is Primary Group of Builders' internal system for tracking company performance against the EOS (Entrepreneurial Operating System) framework: quarterly Revenue, Collections, Expenses, and Disbursements targets versus actuals, quarterly Rocks (90-day priorities), and an executive-level Scorecard and AI-generated analysis that roll all of it up.

This guide covers the Business Unit Integrator role. A Business Unit (BU) Integrator manages one or more specific Business Units end to end — entering actuals, setting targets, and tracking Rocks for the Companies within their assigned Business Unit(s) — without visibility into the rest of the organization.

Note: Every screen in this guide is scoped to whatever Business Units and Companies your account has been granted access to. The screenshots use illustrative figures, not real company data.

2. Signing In

  1. Go to the Executive Dashboard URL your Superadmin gave you.
  2. Enter your email address or username.
  3. Enter your password and select Sign in.

If this is your first time signing in, or a Superadmin has just reset your password, you'll be asked to choose a new password before you can continue — this is a one-time step.

Note: After 5 incorrect password attempts in a row, the app temporarily locks your account for a short period as a security precaution. Wait for the lockout message to clear, or contact a Superadmin.

As a BU Integrator, everything you see is automatically scoped to your assigned Business Unit(s) — the Business Unit and Company filters won't offer an "All" option or any Business Unit you haven't been assigned to. Compare, Reports, and the Audit Log are not part of this role by default; a Superadmin can add access to any of them via a Custom Role if your organization wants to.

  • The sun/moon icon in the top-right switches between light and dark theme.
  • Your name and role/description show in the top-right; click the person icon to open your Profile.
  • The arrow icon next to your name signs you out.
  • « Collapse at the bottom of the sidebar shrinks it to icons-only, useful on smaller screens.

4. Your Access at a Glance

You can view and edit Revenue, Collections, Expenses, Disbursements, and Rocks for the Companies in your assigned Business Unit(s) only. Scorecard, Financials Dashboard, and AI Analysis are available but locked to that same scope. Compare, Reports, and the Audit Log show an "access required" screen unless your organization has specifically granted you one of them.

5. Page-by-Page Walkthrough

5.1 Scorecard

Same Scorecard layout as every other role, but the Business Unit filter is locked to your assigned Business Unit — there's no "All Business Units" option.

Use this to check your Business Unit's full-year revenue attainment, net income, and Rocks completion at a glance before diving into the Financials Dashboard for detail.

5.2 Rocks

Add, update, and track Rocks for any Company in your Business Unit using + Add Rock and the pencil/trash icons.

This is one of your core responsibilities as a BU Integrator — keeping Rocks current is what feeds the Rocks Completion figure on your Business Unit's Scorecard.

5.3 Financials Dashboard

Read-only rollup of your Business Unit's Revenue, Collections, Expenses, and Disbursements — Target Distribution Matrix and Operational Grid both stay limited to your Companies.

Use this to double-check the actuals you've entered on Financials Data Entry are landing correctly before quarter-end.

5.4 Financials Data Entry

This is where you submit each quarter's Revenue, Collections, Expenses, and Disbursements for the Companies you're responsible for.

The Company dropdown only ever shows Companies inside your assigned Business Unit(s) — you cannot submit or override figures for any Company outside your scope.

Use the "Notable Expense/Disbursement Items" lists to log any significant one-off items for record-keeping; they're informational only and never change any total.

5.5 Financials Target Setup

Set quarterly (or annual, split evenly) targets for the Companies in your Business Unit.

Editing one quarter automatically redistributes the difference across that Company's remaining quarters so the annual total stays what it was.

If a quarter shows as locked, a Group Integrator or Superadmin has manually locked it — you'll need to ask them to unlock it before you can make further changes.

5.6 Compare

This role does not include view access to the Compare tab. If you believe you should have access, ask a Superadmin to grant it — either by changing your base role or by adding it to one of your assigned Custom Roles under Admin → Roles.

5.7 Reports

This role does not include view access to Reports. If you believe you should have access, ask a Superadmin to grant it — either by changing your base role or by adding it to one of your assigned Custom Roles under Admin → Roles.

5.8 AI Analysis

Pick a Year and Quarter, then Generate Analysis for an AI-written summary of your Business Unit's Revenue, Collections, Expenses, Rocks, and Disbursements for that period.

The Business Unit filter here is locked to your assignment, same as everywhere else.

5.9 Audit Log

This role does not include view access to the Audit Log. If you believe you should have access, ask a Superadmin to grant it — either by changing your base role or by adding it to one of your assigned Custom Roles under Admin → Roles.

6. Common Workflows

Submitting this quarter's actuals

Open Financials Data Entry, pick the Year/Quarter and the Company, fill in Revenue/Collections/Expenses/Disbursements, and select Save All Figures. Repeat for each Company in your Business Unit.

Keeping Rocks current

Update each Rock's status and progress percentage on the Rocks page as work happens through the quarter, rather than waiting until quarter-end — the Scorecard's Rocks Completion figure reflects whatever is saved there in real time.

Setting next quarter's targets

On Financials Target Setup, either edit the upcoming quarter directly (the remaining quarters absorb the difference automatically) or use Bulk Upload if you're setting targets for many Companies at once.

7. Your Profile

Click the person icon in the top-right to open your Profile. From here you can update your display name, email address, and optional username, and change your password.

Note: Your role, description, and Business Unit/Company assignments are managed by a Superadmin under Admin → Users — you can't change those yourself from your Profile.

8. Tips & FAQ

I don't see a Company I expect to see on Financials Data Entry.

The Company dropdown only shows Companies inside the Business Unit(s) your account is assigned to under Admin → Users. Ask a Superadmin to check your assignment if a Company is missing.

Why can't I open Compare, Reports, or the Audit Log?

Those three aren't part of the Business Unit Integrator role by default. A Superadmin can grant any of them to your account through a Custom Role if your organization needs you to have it.

I edited a quarter's target and the other quarters' numbers changed too — is that a bug?

No — editing one quarter automatically redistributes the difference across that Company's remaining quarters of the same year, so the annual total stays exactly what it was before your edit.
