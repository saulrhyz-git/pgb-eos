Group Integrator User Guide
Primary Group of Builders · Version 1.0

1. Introduction

The EOS Executive Dashboard is Primary Group of Builders' internal system for tracking company performance against the EOS (Entrepreneurial Operating System) framework: quarterly Revenue, Collections, Expenses, and Disbursements targets versus actuals, quarterly Rocks (90-day priorities), and an executive-level Scorecard and AI-generated analysis that roll all of it up.

This guide covers the Group Integrator role. A Group Integrator is the most senior operating role in the app below Superadmin: full view and edit access across every Business Unit and Company, with no scoping restrictions. This is the role typically held by a CEO, COO, or Integrator overseeing the whole organization.

Note: Every screen in this guide is scoped to whatever Business Units and Companies your account has been granted access to. The screenshots use illustrative figures, not real company data.

2. Signing In

  1. Go to the Executive Dashboard URL your Superadmin gave you.
  2. Enter your email address or username.
  3. Enter your password and select Sign in.

If this is your first time signing in, or a Superadmin has just reset your password, you'll be asked to choose a new password before you can continue — this is a one-time step.

Note: After 5 incorrect password attempts in a row, the app temporarily locks your account for a short period as a security precaution. Wait for the lockout message to clear, or contact a Superadmin.

4. Your Access at a Glance

You can view and edit Revenue, Collections, Expenses, Disbursements, and Rocks for every Business Unit and Company in the system, and you have full access to Compare, Reports, and the Audit Log — features that are restricted for the other two roles covered elsewhere in this guide series.

5. Page-by-Page Walkthrough

5.1 Scorecard

The Scorecard is your board-level summary: full-year revenue attainment, net income, and Rocks completion at the top, followed by Annual Target cards and a Full Year Actual vs Target comparison.

Use the Year / Quarter / Business Unit filters at the top to narrow the view — as a Group Integrator you can pick any single Business Unit or leave it on All Business Units for the company-wide picture.

5.2 Rocks

Rocks are this quarter's major priorities per Company. The summary cards show Total Rocks, how many hit their target, how many are on track versus at risk, and the average progress across all of them.

As a Group Integrator you can add, edit, and delete any Rock for any Company using the + Add Rock button and the pencil/trash icons in the table.

  • Filter by Business Unit, Company, or Business Goal to narrow the list.
  • Use Rollover (Group Integrators and Superadmins only) at quarter-end to carry every not-yet-complete Rock forward one quarter.
5.3 Financials Dashboard

Financials Dashboard is a read-only rollup: Revenue, Collections, Expenses, and Disbursements each have their own sub-tab, all sharing the same Year/Quarter/Business Unit/Company filter bar.

The Target Distribution Matrix breaks each Business Unit's target down by quarter; the Operational Grid shows Target vs Actual with attainment percentages, and can be expanded to see each Company within a Business Unit.

5.4 Financials Data Entry

This is where quarterly actuals get recorded — Revenue, Collections, Expenses, and Disbursements for one Company/Year/Quarter at a time, plus optional Remarks per section.

As a Group Integrator you can enter or override figures for any Company, not just ones you're directly assigned to. The "Notable Expense Items" and "Notable Disbursement Items" lists further down the page are informational record-keeping only — they never feed into any dashboard total.

5.5 Financials Target Setup

Set each Company's quarterly targets here, either one quarter at a time (editing one quarter automatically redistributes the difference across that Company's remaining quarters so the annual total holds steady) or as a single Annual Target split evenly across the year.

Bulk Upload lets you set many Companies' targets at once from a spreadsheet.

A quarter that a Group Integrator or Superadmin has manually locked can no longer be edited by anyone until it's unlocked.

5.6 Compare

Compare lets you line up two independently-chosen periods — each with its own Year/Quarter/Business Unit/Company — side by side, with the percentage change between them computed automatically.

Useful for quarter-over-quarter or year-over-year comparisons without re-running the Financials Dashboard twice.

5.7 Reports

Reports re-shapes the same underlying data into flat, exportable tables: Financial Performance (Target vs Actual per Company), Rocks (the full filtered Rock list), and Executive Summary (a per-Business-Unit rollup of both).

Every report can be filtered, previewed on screen, and exported.

5.8 AI Analysis

Pick a Year, Quarter, and Business Unit, then select Generate Analysis for an AI-written executive summary of that period's Revenue, Collections, Expenses, Rocks, and Disbursements, powered by Google Gemini.

The analysis is generated fresh each time from the same figures shown elsewhere in the app — it's a narrative summary, not a separate data source.

5.9 Audit Log

Every meaningful change anywhere in the app — target updates, actuals updates, Rock changes, and more — is recorded here with who made it, when, and a short summary.

Group Integrators and Superadmins have access to the Audit Log by default; filter by action type, user, or date range to find a specific change.

6. Common Workflows

Reviewing this quarter's performance company-wide

Open Scorecard, leave Business Unit on All Business Units, set Quarter to the current quarter, and review the Full Year Revenue Attainment and Rocks Completion cards. Drill into Financials Dashboard for the Business-Unit-level breakdown, or Compare to see it against last quarter.

Closing out a quarter's Rocks

On the Rocks page, update each Company's Rocks to their final status and progress percentage before quarter-end, then use Rollover to carry anything not yet at Target Met forward into the next quarter.

Locking a quarter's targets once they're finalized

On Financials Target Setup, once a quarter's numbers are agreed and shouldn't change, lock it so BU Integrators can no longer edit it — only a Group Integrator or Superadmin can unlock it again later.

7. Your Profile

Click the person icon in the top-right to open your Profile. From here you can update your display name, email address, and optional username, and change your password.

Note: Your role, description, and Business Unit/Company assignments are managed by a Superadmin under Admin → Users — you can't change those yourself from your Profile.

8. Tips & FAQ

I don't see a Business Unit or Company I expect to see.

Your account (or a Custom Role assigned to it) only shows the scope it's been explicitly granted. Ask a Superadmin to check your assignment under Admin → Users.

The numbers on the Financials Dashboard and the Scorecard don't match.

The Scorecard shows Full Year and Year-to-Date figures; the Financials Dashboard can be filtered to a single quarter or All Quarters. Check both are set to the same Year/Quarter/Business Unit before comparing.

Can I still see a Business Unit after removing all of a BU Integrator's access to it?

Yes — Business Unit and Company visibility for a Group Integrator is never restricted by other users' Custom Role assignments; only your own account's scope matters.

Why does a quarter I want to edit say it's locked?

Someone with Group Integrator or Superadmin access has manually locked that quarter under Financials Target Setup. Use the Unlock control there if you need to make a further change.
