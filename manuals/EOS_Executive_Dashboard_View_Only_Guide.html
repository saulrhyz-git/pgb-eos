View-Only User Guide
Primary Group of Builders · Version 1.0

1. Introduction

The EOS Executive Dashboard is Primary Group of Builders' internal system for tracking company performance against the EOS (Entrepreneurial Operating System) framework: quarterly Revenue, Collections, Expenses, and Disbursements targets versus actuals, quarterly Rocks (90-day priorities), and an executive-level Scorecard and AI-generated analysis that roll all of it up.

This guide covers the View-Only role. A View-Only account is built from a Custom Role with View checked and Edit/Delete left unchecked everywhere — typically used for executives, board members, or other stakeholders who need full visibility into performance without the ability to change any figures. Unlike a Business Unit Integrator, a View-Only account is commonly granted visibility across multiple (or all) Business Units, not scoped to just one.

Note: Every screen in this guide is scoped to whatever Business Units and Companies your account has been granted access to. The screenshots use illustrative figures, not real company data.

2. Signing In

  1. Go to the Executive Dashboard URL your Superadmin gave you.
  2. Enter your email address or username.
  3. Enter your password and select Sign in.

If this is your first time signing in, or a Superadmin has just reset your password, you'll be asked to choose a new password before you can continue — this is a one-time step.

Note: After 5 incorrect password attempts in a row, the app temporarily locks your account for a short period as a security precaution. Wait for the lockout message to clear, or contact a Superadmin.

As a View-Only user, the Business Unit filter lets you switch between every Business Unit your Custom Role(s) grant you view access to — often all of them — but every page's Save/Add/Edit controls are non-functional for you: the server rejects any attempt to write data, regardless of what the screen shows. Reports and the Audit Log are not part of this role by default.

  • The sun/moon icon in the top-right switches between light and dark theme.
  • Your name and role/description show in the top-right; click the person icon to open your Profile.
  • The arrow icon next to your name signs you out.
  • « Collapse at the bottom of the sidebar shrinks it to icons-only, useful on smaller screens.

4. Your Access at a Glance

Note: Some pages that accept data entry — Financials Data Entry, Financials Target Setup, and Rocks — still display their normal input fields and Save/Add buttons for every role, including View-Only. This is a known display quirk: the fields are visible, but any attempt to save is rejected on the server with a permission error, because a View-Only Custom Role never grants Edit access. Nothing you can do on these screens will actually change any data.

Outside of that display quirk, your access is exactly what the name implies: you can open and read every page your Custom Role(s) grant View on, across as many Business Units as you've been given, but you cannot add, edit, or delete anything anywhere in the app.

5. Page-by-Page Walkthrough

5.1 Scorecard

Use the Business Unit filter to switch between every Business Unit you've been granted visibility into — full-year revenue attainment, net income, and Rocks completion update to match whichever one you select.

This is usually the best starting page for a View-Only user: one screen per Business Unit covering the headline numbers.

5.2 Rocks

You can filter and browse every Rock across every Business Unit/Company you have view access to, including its status, progress percentage, owner, and remarks.

The + Add Rock button and the pencil/trash icons are visible on screen, but using them will not succeed — your role does not include Rocks edit or delete access.

5.3 Financials Dashboard

The same read-only Target Distribution Matrix and Operational Grid every role sees, switchable across every Business Unit you have access to.

This is the best page for a detailed, category-by-category view of Revenue, Collections, Expenses, and Disbursements without needing Reports access.

5.4 Financials Data Entry

You can open this page and see whatever figures are currently on file for any Company you have view access to, but the input fields and Save All Figures button will not actually record anything you type — the server rejects the save because your Custom Role has no Edit grant on Revenue, Collections, Expenses, or Disbursements.

Note: If you need to correct or add a figure here, ask whoever holds Business Unit Integrator or Group Integrator access for that Company to make the change on your behalf.
5.5 Financials Target Setup

You can see the currently configured Quarter and Annual targets for any Company you have view access to, but Save Quarter Target will not succeed for this role.

5.6 Compare

Line up two periods — each with its own Year/Quarter/Business Unit/Company — side by side. This is a read-only page for every role, so there's nothing different about how a View-Only user experiences it.

5.7 Reports

This role does not include view access to Reports. If you believe you should have access, ask a Superadmin to grant it — either by changing your base role or by adding it to one of your assigned Custom Roles under Admin → Roles.

5.8 AI Analysis

Pick a Year, Quarter, and Business Unit, then Generate Analysis for an AI-written narrative summary — available to switch across every Business Unit you have view access to.

5.9 Audit Log

This role does not include view access to the Audit Log. If you believe you should have access, ask a Superadmin to grant it — either by changing your base role or by adding it to one of your assigned Custom Roles under Admin → Roles.

6. Common Workflows

Getting a quick read on the whole organization

Open Scorecard and step through each Business Unit in the filter dropdown — full-year attainment, net income, and Rocks completion for each, without needing to touch any other page.

Digging into one category's numbers

Open Financials Dashboard and use the Revenue/Collections/Expenses/Disbursements sub-tabs plus the Business Unit/Company filters to drill into whatever specific figure prompted the question.

Comparing this quarter to last

Open Compare, set Period A to the current quarter and Period B to the prior one (same Business Unit), and read the automatically-computed percentage change for each metric.

7. Your Profile

Click the person icon in the top-right to open your Profile. From here you can update your display name, email address, and optional username, and change your password.

Note: Your role, description, and Business Unit/Company assignments are managed by a Superadmin under Admin → Users — you can't change those yourself from your Profile.

8. Tips & FAQ

I typed a number into Financials Data Entry and it looked like it worked — did I just change something?

No. The field will accept your typing, but selecting Save All Figures returns a permission error and nothing is written to the database. Refreshing the page will show the figure is unchanged.

Why do I see input fields and buttons if I can't actually use them?

This is a known display behavior in the current version of the app: pages that support data entry show the same layout to every role, and rely on the server to reject unauthorized saves rather than hiding the controls outright. It's safe — no unauthorized change can actually go through — just visually inconsistent with a strict "view only" expectation.

I need a figure changed — who do I ask?

Whoever holds Business Unit Integrator access for that Company, or a Group Integrator/Superadmin for anything company-wide.
